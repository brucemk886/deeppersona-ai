# Astro quiz v3: love and the other person (2026-10-15)

Owner-approved direction: the target audience is mostly women 18-35 from TikTok. The quiz and the free/paid reading are now about "you and them" (soulmate, the person on your mind), not general self-description.

## Quiz (20 questions, all tap-to-answer; 18 when nobody is on their mind)
gender, drawn_to, status, someone, their_sign*, dob, falls_for (multi), fear (multi), past (multi), when_like, love_lang (multi), future (multi), want_now, their_attitude*, anxious (multi), looks, loved_how, dynamic, effort, destiny.
`*` only shown when `someone` is not "No, not right now". Removed: goals, birth time, birth place, statements, overthink, energy, head/heart, others' needs, compatibility/career yes-no.

Reveal screens: Sun sign (after dob), love type (after when_like), compatibility with their sign or "soulmate sign found" (after anxious), "3 patterns" (before email). Email is validated on the client with the same rules as the server before the loading screen.

## Reading (8 cards)
Free: love type, who you fall for vs. who you need, compatibility with their sign (or soulmate element + score).
Soulmate Report $4.99 (basic): Soulmate Profile, how [their sign] sees you (or what your soulmate notices first), the moment they pull away.
Complete Love Reading $9.99 (full): + top 3 matches & the sign to avoid, next love window.
Locked-card order follows `want_now`. Pronouns follow `drawn_to` (he / she / they).

The love window is computed from Venus' real position (low-precision orbital elements, about 1 day accuracy): the next period Venus is in the user's Sun sign or the opposite sign. The date stays masked until unlocked.

Old v2 snapshots are untouched and still render with the old summary (Sun / Moon / Life Path). Prices are unchanged.
